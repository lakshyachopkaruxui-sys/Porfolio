Check before a pharmacy visit.

Our team selected a health card and app. This new AI-assisted interface explores one of its intended tasks: use a prepared prescription, check a Kendra's availability before travel, then request collection.

This is an interactive concept walkthrough, not the original working app or a live service. Accounts, items, stores and responses are fictional. No real card, prescription, payment or medical data is used.
