A pharmacy-side kiosk flow

Try a small purchase-and-help flow with fictional items. This is a working interface simulation, not a pharmacy service or the original 2025 team work. No real card, PIN, prescription or payment is needed.

One later learning exercise: our team report selected a health card and app. This kiosk exploration broadens our interface-design experience; it does not replace that direction or establish a better service.

    For the moderator · scenarios and observation worksheet

    Scenario controls

    These controls are outside the patient flow. Starting a separate scenario clears this local demo. Status responses are scripted; no service is contacted.

    Manual observation worksheet

    Blank until you record observations. Use anonymous codes. Do not enter names, contact details, PINs, medicine names or health information. Nothing is sent or saved automatically; a page reload clears entries.

    A worksheet is a research aid, not a result. No participants have been tested by this prototype. Consent and any recruitment arrangements must be handled separately.

    English-only exploratory interface. Staff handoff, physical access, language support, card verification, stock, payment and collection services need real-world evaluation. No analytics, cookies, local storage or external SDKs.
