Reading a record is not verifying it.

Our team explored package identity linked to batch, expiry, billing and supporting records. This new November 2026 AI-assisted preview shows the difference between displaying a prepared record and establishing that it is trustworthy.

No RFID hardware, camera, tag permission, manufacturer database, medicine, bill or quality test is connected. This is a concept discussion aid, not an authenticity checker.
