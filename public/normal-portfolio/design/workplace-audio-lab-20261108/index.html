A driver described difficult music/navigation switching and, separately, repeated checking when a song change lagged. This combined task is newly synthesized. The prototype asks whether a clearer action receipt adds value beyond existing shortcuts and feedback.

Stationary desktop exploration. Baleno Zeta / 7-inch SmartPlay Pro is the provisional reference context. This is an original interaction model, not an inspected Maruti screen or Android Auto replica. No real route, vehicle, media service or sound is connected.

What would make this worthwhile?

Understanding

Can someone distinguish an input received locally from playback confirmed by its source, including when the result is unknown?

Less checking

Does the receipt help beyond the existing status and shortcuts, or does it add another thing to read?

Continuity

Can someone leave Media and retain guidance without losing or accidentally repeating an unresolved action?

No winner or improvement is claimed. The task combines related research accounts; exact Maruti hardware, firmware and current screen paths remain to be verified. Real driver sessions are the next evidence step.

Why these controls are here

Google documents media widgets, navigation context and recent-app access. NEXA lists steering-mounted audio controls for the provisional Baleno Zeta context. Both approaches retain these capabilities. The comparison isolates presentation of response state.
